Account and Data Deletion Request

Developer: Echoes of Me Studio

You can request deletion of your Echoes of Me account and its associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Click the button below to contact our support team.
  2. Use the email address associated with your Echoes of Me account.
  3. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject.
  4. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Request Account Deletion

If the button does not work, email: support.echoesofme@gmail.com

What Data Is Deleted?

Following verification and processing of an account deletion request, the account and associated personal data will be deleted, including, where applicable:

  • Account information and identifiers
  • Uploaded photographs
  • Generated images stored by the application
  • Generation history and related account records
  • Subscription entitlement and usage records, subject to applicable retention requirements

Data Retention

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Such information will be retained only as necessary for the applicable purpose.

Third-party service providers may apply their own legally required retention periods.
