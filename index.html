Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Echoes of Me Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Echoes of Me application collects, uses, processes, and protects personal information.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use our application, we may process:

  • Email address and account identifiers.
  • Photographs voluntarily uploaded for AI image generation.
  • Selected image styles, prompts, and generation settings.
  • Generated images and processing status.
  • Subscription status and generation usage limits.
  • Technical information required for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, generate AI-powered images, manage subscription entitlements, enforce generation limits, maintain security, troubleshoot failures, and provide application functionality.

3. Uploaded Photos and AI Processing

When you upload a photograph and request an AI transformation, the image and relevant generation instructions may be transmitted to third-party AI processing services, including Replicate.

Uploaded photographs are used to fulfill the requested image generation. We do not claim ownership of your original photographs.

Photographs may contain personal information about the individuals shown. You should upload only photographs that you have permission to use.

Storage and retention of images may depend on the processing services involved. We do not promise immediate or automatic deletion unless that behavior has been implemented and verified.

4. Authentication and Data Storage

We use Firebase Authentication for account authentication and Supabase for application data storage, image storage, and generation management.

These service providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Where premium subscriptions are available, we may process subscription status, billing periods, and generation allowances. Payments made through Google Play are handled by Google's payment infrastructure. We do not need to store full payment card details for Google Play transactions.

6. Advertising

Free versions of Echoes of Me may display advertisements when advertising functionality is enabled. Advertising providers may process device identifiers and related information according to applicable consent requirements and their privacy policies. This section will be updated to identify the advertising providers actually used.

7. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers necessary to operate the application, including authentication, hosting, storage, AI processing, payments, and, where implemented, advertising services.

We do not sell user-uploaded photographs as photographic content to third parties.

8. Data Retention

We retain account information and service records as necessary for application functionality, security, legal obligations, and legitimate operational purposes.

Uploaded and generated images may remain in application storage until deleted under the applicable data management procedures. Third-party processing services may apply their own retention practices.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting us using the email address below. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law and operational requirements.

Some information may need to be retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

10. Security

We use authentication, access controls, and service-side safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

Echoes of Me is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children under 13.

12. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process and store information in countries other than your country of residence. Applicable data protection laws and safeguards may vary.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing of your personal information, and to raise concerns with the relevant data protection authority.

14. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our technology, service providers, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
